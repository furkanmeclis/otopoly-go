**Description (Apple + Play, 4000):**

```
Otopoly is a business management app for car wash, detailing and auto care shops. It records every step from vehicle drop-off to delivery, so your staff know what to do next and you see how the business is doing in real time.

WORK ORDERS AND OPERATIONS
• Fast vehicle check-in by licence plate
• Service lines, assigned staff and status for every vehicle
• See the In progress → Ready → Delivered flow at a glance
• Schedule multi-day jobs to their delivery day
• Track payment status separately from the job
• Quotes, calendar, customer loyalty programmes and review follow-up

WHATSAPP UPDATES
• Check-in, ready, delivery and payment messages go out automatically from your own business WhatsApp number
• Customers know their car is ready before they call
• Edit message templates in your own words

DIGITAL CONTRACTS
• Have customers approve contracts for jobs such as ceramic coating or PPF on a phone or tablet
• The customer receives a one-time approval code and the privacy notice
• Damage photos are attached and a time-stamped PDF is created

ACCOUNTS, CASH AND INVENTORY
• Jobs on credit are posted to the customer account; see open balances and statements instantly
• Cash and card registers, income and expense categories, transfers between registers
• Product sales; service recipes deduct the products used from stock automatically

REPORTS
• Daily, weekly and monthly income and expenses
• Service mix and top customers

STAFF AND PERMISSIONS
• Give each staff member their own account
• Sensitive actions such as cancellations and finance stay with the owner
• Activity history

BUILT FOR MOBILE
• Home and Lock Screen widgets show today's jobs, the queue and cash at a glance
• Push notifications
• Approve QR sign-in on your computer
• An AI assistant that works with your business data (with your explicit consent)

Otopoly is a service for businesses; you sign in with your account. The app opens in Turkish or English based on your device language.

Privacy policy: https://otopoly.app/en/privacy
```
